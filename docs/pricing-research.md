## Notes
- Competitors charge kameez and shalwar separately; a "suit" price must cover two pieces.
- Our Rs. 1,000 free-delivery threshold is 4x more generous than the market. Check the rider cost per trip: an Rs. 1,000 order may not cover a free pickup + delivery in Malir or Gulzar-e-Hijri.
- Common market offer: 10% off the first order.
