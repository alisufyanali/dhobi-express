# Real photos

Put real photos here and re-run the scripts — they replace the illustrations automatically.

**Posts 01–16** (`node posts.mjs`): name the photo after the post, e.g. `01-wash-iron.jpg`, `02-dry-clean.jpg`, `07-bedsheets.jpg`.
Shown in the round white plate, so a square photo with the item in the middle works best.

**Problem → Solution posts 17–25** (`node problems.mjs`): two photos per post, `<key>-before.jpg` and `<key>-after.jpg`.
Keys: `stain`, `wrinkle`, `water`, `power`, `pile`, `rain`, `bridal`, `collar`, `time` — e.g. `stain-before.jpg`, `stain-after.jpg`.

Any of .jpg / .jpeg / .png / .webp works.
